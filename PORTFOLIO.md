# Portfolio Blurbs

Copy-paste versions for GitHub, LinkedIn, or a personal site.

## 1. CareerCompass AI

An n8n automation that scrapes Hacker News "Who is hiring?" posts, uses GPT-5.6 Sol to extract and score jobs against my profile, drafts a personalized cover letter for strong matches, upserts them to Google Sheets, and emails me a daily digest. Human-in-the-loop: AI prepares, I apply.

## 2. SupportPilot AI

A webhook-powered AI support agent that classifies tickets, drafts replies, estimates confidence, logs everything to Google Sheets, and escalates low-confidence or urgent cases to a human team. Designed to augment support reps, not replace them.

## 3. OutreachEngine AI

An outreach automation that reads leads from Google Sheets, fetches each lead's website, and writes a personalized cold email using only facts found on the site. If the site is thin or broken, it flags the lead for human review instead of hallucinating. Emails become Gmail drafts - nothing sends automatically.

## 4. LedgerLens AI

An invoice pipeline that takes a PDF upload, extracts vendor, line items, totals, and payment terms with structured LLM output, logs it to Google Sheets, and notifies finance. The pattern extends to receipts, purchase orders, and compliance documents.

## 5. ActionFlow AI

An automation that turns meeting transcripts into concise summaries, key decisions, risks, and owner-assigned action items. Each action item becomes a tracked row in Google Sheets, and the team gets an email digest.
